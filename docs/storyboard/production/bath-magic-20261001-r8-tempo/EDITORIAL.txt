2. The first duck voice has a clean reveal58 and room for disbelief59–61, play62–64, then multiplication66 and the hidden-twelfth payoff68. The child's surprise and the baby's answer give different reactions instead of repeated smiles.
3. Macro pacing genuinely accelerates in image space. Early practical establishment is expansive; the last three mishaps begin only four images apart. The giant pot76, bubbles83 and floating breakfast104 are distinct physical set pieces.

5. Energetic family behavior is compatible with rhythm. Wonder, precision, a raised eyebrow, counting, physical effort and silence provide contrast without exhausted faces or frightening escalation.

CURRENT WEAKNESSES — EDITORIAL JUDGMENT
1. The opening is much longer than any later day.31–44 spans exit, bedroom protection, channel construction, route checks, cleanup, sleep, waking, breakfast and another leak/cloth task before a new magical development45. The practical geography is valuable, but equal emphasis on every image could feel like a procedural plateau after the splash.
2.53–57 stacks supper, bed, toast, bucket and another attempt.40–44 does a similar reset. The images are legitimate family life; the danger is repeated narrative restarting rather than image count itself.
3. The recalled clue at70 makes PinPin's long delay until114 more conspicuous than the initial clue50 alone would. He spends44 intervening image positions trying other spells after explicitly remembering the useful direction. A viewer may ask why he does not test it then. This is the most important learning-arc weakness.
4. The later causal links are now intelligible, but causal connection is not automatically dramatic necessity. A wet cloth87, wet belongings92 and a breakfast cloth103 can look manageable without another spell. If every attempted fix is presented with the same confidence and weight, the middle risks becoming a catalogue of tricks despite the DAG.
5. Local problems resolve rapidly and repeatedly: plant placed78, bubbles dispersed86, cloud moved91, towels settled96, slippers dried101, furniture lowered106. This gives satisfying competence but also lets pressure fall almost to baseline after each episode. The original source must remain perceptually present or the final reversal can feel like solving an old background issue rather than the current central objective.
6.112 already brings the uphill water back down. That partially resolves the preceding visual spectacle before the real solution.114–121 must therefore become an attention/clue climax rather than pretending that danger or physical scale keeps increasing. The smallest image can carry the largest realization.

CHART DESIGN ADVICE
A defensible report should separate at least three tracks:
A. Measured events: day boundaries, exact spell attempts, misfires, local containment, source-stop121.
B. Annotated narrative state: which consequence is active, managed, or permanently retained. A duck moving into a pool is management, not an undo; the flower garden is a new equilibrium.
C. Editorial interpretation: proposed energy, attention and release curves, explicitly labeled subjective. Do not combine all three into a fake measured “tension” line.
Show current versus proposed emphasis with the SAME126 positions. A smoother idealized contour is a chosen editorial target, not evidence that a studio uses that curve.
Do not infer reading speed from one image per position. Actual time depends on narration, scrolling, child attention and how long viewers linger.
Most useful single diagnostic: spell-attempt gaps alongside local-resolution markers. It reveals both the opening plateau and the later accelerating reset pattern without inventing audience data.

PRIMARY CONCLUSION
The chapter has a convincing accelerating comic structure and an earned final experiment. Its main opportunity is not fewer requested images or more distress: it is unequal emphasis. Give the long practical opening montage momentum, vary the later refrain cycles, and make the remembered clue develop rather than appear postponed. Let the final small, quiet observation outrank the earlier giant spectacles.
