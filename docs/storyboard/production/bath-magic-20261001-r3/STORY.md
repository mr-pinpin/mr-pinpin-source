# Bath chapter R3 — shared family splash

Complete 62-scene EN/RU/ES text/shot plan, 38 exact R2 story reuses, 24 new story illustrations plus new cover. R2 stays untouched. All 63 images are internally reviewed selected drafts; user feedback pending, not published.

The bath becomes a shared playful family experience: Papa truly jumps, Mama joins holding the infant, PinPin joins, and the camera goes underwater. Two failed attempts to stop the water first produce flowers, then an unannounced talking-toy surprise. The child still finds the reversal himself and the family still cleans together.
