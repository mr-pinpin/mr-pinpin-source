## The story

Papa takes Scooby for a real morning walk and practice, while Mama cares for
PomPom and PinPin's play begins with Rabbit. Scooby follows a cool draft and a
strange blossom to a root-covered entrance in the forest. Behind it is a connected
cave system: moss, hanging vines, softly glowing berries, reflected pools, flowers
and warm patches of light. The atmosphere takes inspiration from lush caves;
the book retains its established dimensional storybook art, not a voxel/game UI.

The discovery becomes a four-day family adventure. PinPin and Rabbit want to know
where the water goes; Papa helps observe and mark a route; the Elder offers a
clue rather than the answer; Beaver understands the forest outlet. Mama and
PomPom participate in the broad, sunlit entrance/picnic chambers, with PomPom
always supported. Small sounds, echoes, light and familiar routines give the
infant meaningful reactions and the mother an active care thread.

Scooby's useful nose opens the adventure, but damp earth, clay, brushing past wet
roots and a final joyful muddy roll make him thoroughly dirty. The practiced
"wait" cue has real payoffs at the cave threshold and later during care. He is
never trapped in a deep pit. **No staircase, rescue construction, ladder or
arbitrary engineering detour.** The cave entrance itself is the interesting find;
the lost wooden-boat idea is superseded. Two familiar leaf boats belong to the
children's water-observation/play subplot, with their counts and holders tracked.

The forest bath is an explicitly prepared **magical tree shower**. The family
chooses the tree and watches a small test before the final wash. PinPin reuses
the golden water circle and the reverse stopping motion he learned in Bath
Magic. A single source at the chosen overhead leaf makes water fall through the
canopy. Parents check the gentle flow/temperature before Scooby goes beneath it.
Soap, brush, two towels and a mat are visibly packed and carried there. There is
no unexplained full wooden bath appearing in the forest. The circle is visibly
stopped after testing and after washing; no unattended overnight flow.

Papa and PinPin wash Scooby carefully, including his paws, rinse completely,
survive his enormous shake, dry and brush him, then return to the familiar home.
The house is already his home; this is daily family care, not earning membership.
The final doorway shows the whole dog and family at the prior chapter's scale,
with one correct green door and the familiar room. Mama's evening routine,
PomPom's settling, the children's account of the cave, yawns and Scooby's cushion
close all the threads. Sleep means letting an active day's body and mind rest.

## Connected threads

1. **Papa/Scooby:** sit → wait → release → nose leads elsewhere → threshold wait
   works → dog helps discovery → dirty curiosity → consent to wash → settled rest.
2. **PinPin/Rabbit:** leaf-boat play → puzzling direction/sound → return by a shown
   path → observe cave water → test rather than assume → share the discovery;
   invitations and promises are followed through, not abandoned for days.
3. **Mama/PomPom:** meals, support/handovers and shared play → picnic preparation
   → infant's sensory/echo moments → daily clean-paw routine → shower preparation
   and warm-water check → dry-side reactions → familiar bedtime care.
4. **Water/place:** cool draft, blossom and visible entrance → lit chambers and
   branching streams → route clues and outlet → falling canopy water inspires
   controlled reuse of known magic → explicit reverse/last drop/quiet leaf.
5. **Supporting cast:** two canonical birds give a small spring beat; Elder and
   Beaver each contribute one useful observation; Rabbit is an ongoing partner.
   Cast appearances must have a reason and a next/last location, not cameo filler.

## Acting, cast and dirt

The warm style is a palette/material choice, not an instruction for everyone to
smile. Each visible character has its own gaze, brow, mouth, ears/posture and
emotional turn. Default smiles are removed. Earned pleasure is allowed; concentration,
curiosity, puzzlement, quiet awe, listening, mild firmness, wry annoyance, surprise,
reluctance, comfort and sleep must be visibly different.

Papa stays a broad adult with rounded muzzle/grey-tipped quills. Mama is the
russet-quilled mother. PinPin is the older child; PomPom is a distinct infant and
always held/supported, never walking or submerged. Scooby is the published brown,
cream-chested mount-sized dog: withers near Papa's standing head on common ground,
head higher; no new toy-dog redesign or enormous isolated foreground paw. Rabbit,
the two birds, Elder and Beaver use the actual prior character references.

Days1–3 have explicitly tracked incidental soil/wet paws and ordinary evening
cleaning. Day4's major dirt accumulates on **all four legs, paws, belly, chest,
flank and parts of the back** and persists until actual washing. It never resets
to decorative ankle spots. Wetness, mud, suds, water flow, towels and dry coat are
separate states. The last rinse removes the last suds before the dog steps clear.
