# Acting direction and editorial gates

No image is approved merely because it rendered. BEAT-PLAN acting fields are
initial register targets; final shot direction must choose one specific gaze,
mouth/brow, body action and reaction for every actor before generation.

Review each adjacent pair for time/location, cast entrances, holders, mud/soap,
flow and camera scale. Review complete day boards for acting repetition. Neutral
mouths are not a substitute for acting either: gestures must carry the beat.

## Audit of published short chapter

The V3 technical checks established fast loading and downloads, not story/art
quality. Editorial issues: short breakfast/play setup, absent PomPom, missing
actual Papa training, weak dig discovery, improvised stairs, dirt resetting,
unintroduced forest bath water, inconsistent jug/bottle positions, suds still
visible after purported final rinse, room/door scale drift and excessive repeated
smiles. Its shake and yawning shots do show useful differentiated reactions;
retain that lesson, without calling the full sequence approved for R6.
