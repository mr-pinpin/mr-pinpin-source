  not five loose objects. This reads clearly in a child's small hand and can be
  matched to the flowering shrub over C0. It returns to C0 on day2. It is not the

  distinct from this large pink garden flower.

  Cabinets left/stove right in interior view reverse when viewed from outside

  conformance claim. The separate measured model is unchanged.
