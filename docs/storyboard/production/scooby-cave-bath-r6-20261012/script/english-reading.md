# Scooby Gets a Bath — cave adventure English script draft

Authoring draft; not the published reader.

## Day 1

**001 · Warm kitchen**

The kitchen smelled of warm bread. Mama stirred the porridge. Papa had PomPom tucked against his arm, and PinPin was listening to the spoon go round.

**002 · Four bowls**

Four bowls stood on the round table. “A little breakfast first,” said Mama. “There is a whole day waiting outside.”

**003 · Bread exchange**

Papa broke the bread with his free paw and passed a piece to PinPin. PomPom watched the piece travel across the table.

**004 · Tiny spoon reply**

Tap. PomPom touched his spoon to his bowl. Mama answered with a little tap of her own. PomPom waited—and tried it again.

**005 · Cleanup and handover**

After breakfast, PinPin gathered the bowls. Papa settled PomPom into Mama’s arms. The spoon tapping had finished; the washing up had begun.

**006 · Ball rolls**

Mama sat on the rug with PomPom supported against her. With her free paw, she rolled the soft ball toward PinPin. It bumped gently against his foot.

**007 · Infant touches ball**

PinPin brought the ball back. PomPom stretched out a tiny paw and touched it. “Your turn,” whispered PinPin, though the ball needed a little help to roll.

**008 · Papa announces walk**

“Scooby and I are going for a walk,” said Papa. “We have some practising to do.” PinPin looked toward the door. Practising what?

**009 · Clear place to practise**

Scooby had finished breakfast. Papa eased the big pink flowerpot to the side of the step, making a clear place to practise. Scooby lifted his head. His tail made one hopeful sweep.

**010 · Sit cue**

“Sit,” said Papa. Scooby folded his back legs and sat. Papa’s paw stayed still. Scooby watched it carefully.

**011 · Watch the hand**

There was something interesting beyond the fence. Scooby’s nose turned a little. Then his eyes came back to Papa’s quiet hand.

**012 · Wait cue**

“Wait.” Papa stepped away. Scooby stayed where he was. Waiting was much harder when a whole forest was full of smells.

**013 · Release and walk**

“All right. Come along.” Scooby rose at once. Together they followed the path out of the garden, one pair of little feet and four very large paws.

**014 · Mama's small care**

Inside, Mama wiped a spot of porridge from PomPom’s cheek. “Every day we eat, every day we play,” she said. “And every day we take care of ourselves.”

**015 · Knock**

Knock, knock. PinPin stopped beside the ball. Mama turned toward the sound, keeping PomPom snug against her.

**016 · Rabbit on the step**

Rabbit stood on the step with two folded green leaves. “I kept our boats,” said Rabbit. One ear leaned hopefully toward PinPin.

**017 · Invitation**

“Shall we try them at the spring?” asked Rabbit. PinPin looked at the little leaf boats. Then he looked back at Mama.

**018 · Permission and route**

“You may,” she said. “The spring path, and home again afterward.” PinPin nodded. Rabbit was already checking that neither leaf had lost its folded edge.

**019 · Children take the fork**

At the fork, the children took the path toward the spring. Rabbit carried one boat; PinPin carried the other. The old birch stood between the two paths.

**020 · Papa takes forest side**

Papa and Scooby had taken the forest side. The dog walked slowly now. There were ferns to sniff, roots to sniff, and a particularly interesting bit of air.

**021 · Window wave**

From the round window, Mama watched the little travellers disappear behind the leaves. PomPom waved a paw at the patch of light where they had been.

**022 · Nose catches a scent**

Scooby stopped. His nose hovered just above the ferns. He took a long sniff, and then another, shorter one.

**023 · Papa notices**

Papa stopped too. “What have you found?” Scooby did not answer. His ears had turned toward a shadow beneath the roots.

**024 · Cool draft and blossom**

Cool air came from that shadow. It stirred one pale lilac blossom on the ground. The blossom trembled, although the rest of the glade was still.

**025 · Loose litter moves**

Scrape, scrape. Scooby pushed loose leaves aside. Dark earth clung to his front paws. The cool smell grew stronger.

**026 · An entrance**

Behind the leaves was an opening. Roots curved over it like the fingers of an enormous tree. This was no little hole for a buried stick.

**027 · Wait at threshold**

Scooby lowered his head to go farther. Papa lifted his paw. “Wait.” Scooby stopped, with his nose almost inside.

**028 · Check the first ground**

Papa looked at the broad ground beyond the roots. Daylight reached a little way in. He could see moss, pale stone, and a dry place to stand.

**029 · Spring established**

At the spring, water whispered around the familiar stones. PinPin and Rabbit knelt beside it, each with a leaf boat ready.

**030 · Bird greeting**

The little blue-and-yellow bird perched above them. The robin came nearer the water. “We remember you,” said PinPin softly. Neither bird seemed in a hurry.

**031 · Adjust leaf edges**

Rabbit pinched the leaf’s folded corner. PinPin copied the fold. The boats were still the same two boats, but a good boat sometimes needed a little mending.

**032 · First leaf released**

PinPin set his boat on the water. It slipped away from his paw and followed the narrow current.

**033 · Second turns**

Rabbit’s boat took a different turn. Round it went, behind a stone. “Mine is coming back,” said Rabbit, puzzled.

**034 · Bird watches the turn**

The robin tilted its head. The blue-and-yellow bird watched from the branch. For a moment, everyone was looking at the same little turn of water.

**035 · A call from the path**

“PinPin!” Papa’s voice came from the forest path. The children lifted both boats out. They would find out about that turn another time.

**036 · Return through fork**

They went back to the fork, past the old birch, and took the other path. Rabbit kept the damp boat cupped between both paws.

**037 · Paths rejoin**

Papa was waiting in the fern glade. Scooby was waiting too, although his nose was doing a great deal of work.

**038 · Papa explains draft**

“Look at the ferns,” said Papa. A small frond quivered beside the roots. Rabbit’s ears leaned forward. PinPin crouched lower.

**039 · PinPin looks closely**

PinPin looked into the opening. He remembered the cool passage beneath the Elder’s house. “There are more rooms under the forest?” he whispered.

**040 · One fallen blossom taken**

He picked up the fallen blossom. It was soft and pale lilac. “I wonder where you came from,” he said.

**041 · First chamber glimpse**

They took a few steps onto the daylight floor. Beyond them, moss softened the stone. Farther in, small amber berries glimmered among hanging vines.

**042 · A small echo**

“Hello,” said Papa quietly. “Hello,” the cave gave back, a little softer. Rabbit turned one ear toward it. Scooby looked behind Papa.

**043 · Meanwhile at home**

At home, PomPom was reaching for his clean cloth. Mama steadied his little body. “There it is,” she murmured. Some discoveries were small enough to hold.

**044 · Come prepared tomorrow**

“We’ll come back prepared,” said Papa. “And we’ll tell Mama.” Scooby glanced deeper into the cave, then followed Papa back into the daylight.

**045 · Remember the birch**

PinPin looked once more at the forked birch and the pale root beside the entrance. Tomorrow, they would know where to come.

**046 · Homeward path**

They walked home along the same forest path. The two boats were damp. The blossom was dry. Scooby’s front paws were not quite as clean as when he had left.

**047 · Rabbit's farewell**

At the cottage, PinPin gave his leaf back to Rabbit. “Tomorrow?” “Tomorrow,” said Rabbit. Rabbit would keep both boats damp until then.

**048 · Tell Mama**

PinPin laid the blossom on a little cloth by the table. “Scooby found a room under the roots,” he told Mama. “And the room said hello.”

**049 · Wipe dog paws**

Before Scooby came in, Papa wiped the earth from his paws with the sage-coloured dog cloth. One paw, then the next. Scooby waited.

**050 · Rinse the used cloth**

Papa rinsed the dog cloth in the washing basin. Mama’s cream cloth stayed beside PomPom. Some things needed their own little places.

**051 · Family settles**

That night, all four hedgehogs settled into their broad bed. “Every day we play,” Mama murmured, “and every night we rest.” PinPin was still thinking of the cool air.

**052 · Scooby rests**

In the common room, Scooby lay on his cushion. His nose twitched once. Then even his busy nose went to sleep.
## Day 2

**053 · New morning**

Morning light reached the quilt. PinPin opened his eyes. Today, he knew exactly what he wanted to tell Rabbit.

**054 · Discovery remembered**

At breakfast, the lilac blossom lay beside the table. “We heard an echo,” said PinPin. PomPom tapped his spoon. “You would like it,” Mama told him.

**055 · Picnic cloth packed**

Mama held PomPom while PinPin helped fold the picnic cloth around the food and the baby’s small spoon. “A long look needs a little lunch,” she said.

**056 · Flask and dog bowl**

Papa filled the flask and packed three cups. Scooby’s bowl fitted beside them. A folded sheet of paper and a pencil went into the bag’s small pocket.

**057 · Three marker strips**

“Three strips,” said Papa. He laid three little ochre cloth strips on the table. “For the turns we want to remember.” PinPin counted them with his paw.

**058 · Check the bag**

At the step, Papa checked the bag. PinPin tucked the blossom into a small fold of cloth. Mama held PomPom close. Scooby looked at the forest path.

**059 · Rabbit returns**

Rabbit arrived with both leaves. “I kept them damp,” said Rabbit. “They haven’t forgotten how to be boats.”

**060 · Leaf condition**

PinPin lifted one leaf to look at its corners. Rabbit straightened the other. Neither boat looked quite new, but both were ready.

**061 · Scooby lowers**

Scooby lowered himself beside the step. Mama looked at Papa. PomPom looked at Scooby’s very large ear.

**062 · Infant handover for mounting**

Papa took PomPom into his arms. With both paws free, Mama settled onto Scooby’s broad back. Scooby stayed still.

**063 · PomPom back to Mama**

Papa placed PomPom safely against Mama again. She held him close. “Slowly, please,” she told Scooby, though he was already waiting.

**064 · Gentle family departure**

They set out together. Mama and PomPom rode at a gentle walk. Papa carried the bag; PinPin and Rabbit walked beside him, with one leaf boat each.

**065 · Fork recognised**

At the fork, PinPin pointed. “That way for the spring. This way for the roots.” Rabbit nodded. The birch had not moved overnight.

**066 · Birch landmark**

The fern glade looked different with morning sunshine on it. Then PinPin saw the forked pale root. “There,” he said. Scooby had already found it with his nose.

**067 · Wait and dismount**

At the entrance, Papa took PomPom while Mama stepped down. Then he returned the baby to her arms. Scooby waited through it all.

**068 · First route strip**

Papa tied the first ochre strip around the dry root beside the entrance. “Our way out,” he said. Rabbit looked back at the forest before they went in.

**069 · Blossom returned**

PomPom touched the lilac blossom. Mama pointed to the flowers above the entrance. “The same colour.” PinPin laid it on the moss. “You belong here,” he whispered.

**070 · C1 wide**

Inside was a broad, bright room. Ferns stood beside the dry path. Along the back wall, a little stream made its way toward a darker arch.

**071 · Fern attention**

PomPom reached toward a fern. Mama brought him near enough to look. He opened his paw, closed it, and watched the frond sway.

**072 · Paws stay on path**

Papa led Scooby along the firm path. Damp moss brushed the edges of the dog’s paws. “This way,” said Papa. The path was wider than it had looked outside.

**073 · Listen to drops**

Plip. Plip. The children stood still. Water was dropping somewhere ahead, and the cave made each little sound feel important.

**074 · Glowberry gallery first view**

Beyond the pale boulder, the path bent into the hanging vines. Amber berries glowed above the moss. PinPin slowed down without being asked.

**075 · Light on faces**

“Who put lights in them?” whispered Rabbit. PinPin looked up. He had seen lamps underground before. These little lights had leaves.

**076 · Mother's daylight station**

Back in the bright first room, Mama settled with PomPom. A stripe of daylight warmed the picnic place. She could still hear the others along the path.

**077 · Back for family**

The little scouting party came back. “There’s a whole gallery,” said PinPin. “With lights that grow.” Papa set down the bag. First, lunch.

**078 · Picnic cloth unfolds**

Papa held PomPom while Mama spread the picnic cloth. Rabbit helped PinPin flatten its corners. Nobody wanted a cup on a fold.

**079 · Food placed**

Food came out of the bag, then the cups, then Scooby’s bowl. The small bright room began to feel like somewhere they had meant to be.

**080 · PomPom's small meal**

Mama held PomPom against her and offered a little spoonful. He watched the spoon, then her face. The cave could wait for one small mouthful.

**081 · Scooby drinks**

Papa poured water into Scooby’s bowl. The dog drank slowly. His nose had found a great many things, but his mouth had found something simple.

**082 · Children recount glow**

“Perhaps the berries are lamps,” said PinPin. “But who turns them off?” asked Rabbit. Papa listened. That was a good question to keep.

**083 · Elder arrives by entrance**

A familiar voice came through the roots. The Elder had heard their voices from the forest path. His round spectacles caught a little entrance light.

**084 · Tell Elder**

“We have questions,” said PinPin. “So I can hear,” said the Elder. Mama made room beside the picnic cloth.

**085 · Elder's clue**

“Here the lights grow on vines,” said the Elder. “You can leave them growing.” PinPin thought of the lamps beneath the Elder’s house. These were different rooms—and different lights.

**086 · A small paper map**

Papa took the folded paper and pencil from the bag. “We could draw what we have seen.” The Elder held one corner while PinPin unfolded it.

**087 · Draw what we saw**

PinPin drew the entrance and the bright room. Rabbit showed him where the path had bent. Beyond their little marks, the paper was still empty.

**088 · Echo pool approached**

After lunch, they followed the vine gallery farther. It opened into a tall room with a shallow pool and a pale curved bank.

**089 · A spoken word returns**

“Here?” whispered Rabbit. “Here,” answered the room. PinPin pressed his lips together and listened for the last little bit of the word.

**090 · Mother/infant at broad lip**

Papa went back for Mama and PomPom. They came along the broad path, past the hanging vines, to the dry lip of the pool.

**091 · PomPom's echo**

“Oo,” said PomPom. “Oo,” said the room. PomPom stopped moving. Something had answered him.

**092 · Pause after echo**

Mama looked at her baby. Rabbit looked at PinPin. Papa waited. Then PomPom tried again, and this time a small smile followed the sound.

**093 · Two branches seen**

Mama carried PomPom back to the picnic place. Beyond the pool, Papa and the children found a root dividing the stream. One run went straight around it; the other curled away behind.

**094 · Mark second root**

Papa tied the second strip on the dry side of the fork. “Another turn to remember.” Rabbit studied the water. Could both runs go to the same place?

**095 · Flowers beyond**

Through the next arch, soft lilac blossoms hung in a shaft of daylight. Beneath them were mossy islands and shallow clay-coloured pools.

**096 · Observe without picking**

PinPin recognised the colour. “Like the blossom Scooby found.” Rabbit bent to look, keeping his paws away from the flowers.

**097 · Scooby checks ground**

Scooby sniffed beside a wet root. That same cool, earthy smell was stronger here. A little clay stuck to his paw.

**098 · Turn back deliberately**

“Far enough for today,” said Papa. They turned back past the second strip. The flowers and the two streams could keep their questions until tomorrow.

**099 · Collect picnic items**

At the picnic place, they gathered the three cups, the flask and Scooby’s bowl. Mama held PomPom while the others folded the cloth.

**100 · Map into bag**

Papa put the unfinished map back in its pocket. “Some of the paper is still empty,” said PinPin. “That is what questions are for,” said the Elder.

**101 · Farewell to Elder**

Outside, the Elder turned toward his own path. “Bring me your map another day,” he said. PinPin promised he would.

**102 · Family path home**

Everyone walked home. Mama held PomPom; Papa carried the bag. Scooby’s paws made damp marks, but his back stayed clean.

**103 · Rabbit leaves with leaves**

Rabbit took both leaves home again. “Tomorrow we’ll see where the water goes.” PinPin looked at the folded map in Papa’s hand. Tomorrow had a shape already.

**104 · Paw cloth routine**

At the step, Papa wiped Scooby’s paws. Mama watched with PomPom against her. “Every day we look after one another,” she said.

**105 · Map beside supper**

Beside supper, the map lay open. PinPin showed Mama the stream that split. PomPom made a soft “oo,” but this room did not answer him twice.

**106 · Quiet beds**

The quilt settled over four sleepy hedgehogs. In the other room, Scooby settled on his cushion. The little map waited quietly on its shelf.
## Day 3

**107 · Day3 morning**

The next morning, PinPin looked at the map before he looked outside. There was a bend he could remember—and a bend he could not yet draw.

**108 · Map question**

“Does the water stop behind the root?” he asked Papa. “We saw it disappear,” said Papa. “That is not quite the same thing.”

**109 · Mother checks small supplies**

Mama checked the food and refilled flask while Papa held PomPom nearby. Then the baby came back into her arms. They were ready for another long look.

**110 · Rabbit and leaves**

Rabbit arrived with the two leaves. “They can go where our paws don’t fit,” said Rabbit. PinPin looked from the boats to the blank part of the map.

**111 · Forest fork again**

Together they followed the forest path. Papa carried the bag. The children carried one leaf each. Mama walked with PomPom held close.

**112 · Marker1 still there**

The first ochre strip was still beside the entrance. Rabbit touched it lightly. It was good to find something exactly where you remembered it.

**113 · Sunlit family station**

In the daylight room, Mama and PomPom settled beside the picnic cloth. “Come back and tell us,” she said. Papa nodded before the others followed the inner arch.

**114 · PomPom watches reflection**

A bright reflection moved across the stone. PomPom followed it with his eyes. When a ripple changed its shape, his tiny paw opened in surprise.

**115 · Return to echo pool**

Past the vine gallery, the echo pool was waiting. PinPin could recognise the pale curved bank now, even before he heard a word return.

**116 · Listen before guessing**

They listened first. Water whispered behind the roots. “There,” said Rabbit, very quietly. Papa looked where Rabbit was looking.

**117 · Marker2 and fork**

At the second strip, they could see both little runs. “One boat each,” said PinPin. Rabbit crouched beside the slower-looking branch.

**118 · First leaf test**

PinPin released his leaf into the straight run. It passed under the root’s edge, and for a moment they could not see it.

**119 · Second leaf test**

Rabbit set his boat on the curling run. It turned gently, nose first, toward the bend.

**120 · Watch their directions**

The children watched from the broad bank. One leaf had vanished; the other was still moving. Neither child was ready to guess yet.

**121 · One slows**

Rabbit’s leaf turned round in a little eddy. Rabbit’s ears drew together. “It’s doing what it did at the spring.”

**122 · Look around the root**

Papa pointed beyond the root. “Look where the narrow bit opens.” A little ripple freed Rabbit’s leaf. They followed it along the dry bank.

**123 · Other leaf appears**

In the flower room, PinPin’s leaf appeared beneath a root rib. A moment later, Rabbit’s leaf followed it. Two ways had brought them to one place.

**124 · Gather both leaves**

They lifted both leaves onto the flat stone. Rabbit checked his folded corner. “You took your time,” Rabbit told the boat.

**125 · Map amended**

PinPin added the two runs to the map. Rabbit traced their meeting place with one paw. The empty paper was becoming a place they knew.

**126 · Scooby notices daylight**

Scooby raised his nose. Beyond the flowers, the air smelled warmer. One ear turned toward a faint rustle of forest leaves.

**127 · Toward brighter roots**

They followed the broad bank toward that light. The cave ceiling opened into roots again. Beyond the roots was daylight.

**128 · Outlet established**

Outside, the stream slipped past a pale leaning root. The forest was on this side of the hill too. Rabbit looked back through the opening.

**129 · Third strip**

Papa tied the third and last strip beside the outlet. “Our other way out.” PinPin looked at the stream going on without them.

**130 · Return with the news**

They returned through the flower room, the fork and the echo pool to Mama. PinPin unfolded the map. “There are two entrances!”

**131 · Infant response**

PomPom reached toward PinPin’s pointing paw. “And two ways for the boats,” PinPin told him. PomPom held one finger, as if that was quite enough for now.

**132 · Outside stream**

The family went outside and followed the forest path around the hill. At the other entrance, they found the same stream coming out among the roots.

**133 · Beaver at stream**

Beaver was beside the water. He turned when the children called. “Have you found something?” he asked.

**134 · Tell Beaver observations**

“A stream inside the hill,” said Rabbit. “It splits and comes back together.” PinPin showed Beaver the two lines on the map.

**135 · Beaver's limited clue**

Beaver looked downstream. “This bit carries on toward the lake,” he said. “You can follow it from here.” He pointed to the bank, not to the paper.

**136 · Follow the clue**

So they followed the bank. Under roots, around stones, through an open patch of reeds: the water kept moving.

**137 · Lake glimpse**

Then the trees opened, and there was the lake. PinPin recognised it before anyone said its name.

**138 · Known ducks if shown**

Out on the water were the ducks they knew: two grown ducks and ten little ones. They turned toward the familiar voices. There was still plenty of room to swim.

**139 · Observation shared**

“It went somewhere while we couldn’t see it,” said PinPin. Papa nodded. “Now we know a little more.” There were still things they did not know.

**140 · Birds and fallen drops**

On the way back around the hill, a drop fell from a low spreading branch. The robin shook its feathers. The blue-and-yellow bird hopped onto a drier twig.

**141 · Choose this tree**

“A shower,” said PinPin, looking up. Mama glanced at Scooby’s damp paws. “A very small one,” she said. Papa looked at the flat ground beneath the branch.

**142 · Known circle recalled**

PinPin remembered the golden circle. He remembered the water that kept coming, too. “I know how to stop it now,” he said.

**143 · Reach the chosen leaf**

The branch was low enough for Papa to steady PinPin beneath it. The child could reach the air just below one broad leaf. “A little test,” said Papa.

**144 · Small incomplete arc**

PinPin traced a golden arc. The line was not closed yet. Everyone waited. No water fell.

**145 · Circle closes**

The circle joined. A gentle stream fell below the leaf. Papa lowered PinPin onto the dry ground, and Rabbit took one small step back.

**146 · Check falling flow**

Papa put a paw at the edge of the falling water. Mama watched from the dry side with PomPom. “Gentle,” said Papa. “And pleasantly warm.”

**147 · Watch where it goes**

The drops met the earth and ran away toward the lower moss. “It doesn’t need to fill a bath,” said PinPin. Papa checked that it ran away from the entrance.

**148 · Reverse starts**

Papa steadied PinPin within reach again. The child moved his paw all the way around the circle in reverse. This part mattered just as much as starting it.

**149 · Thin stream**

The golden line faded. The little stream thinned to a thread. Scooby watched the last of it, with his head tilted.

**150 · Last drop and quiet leaf**

One drop fell. Then the leaf was quiet. Papa set PinPin down. Mama looked up once more before anyone left.

**151 · Beaver farewell**

Before going home, they called goodbye to Beaver by the stream. “Thank you for showing us the bank,” said Rabbit. Beaver lifted a paw in reply.

**152 · Homeward route**

They took the familiar path home. The source was off. The two leaf boats were back in their paws, and the map had one more line.

**153 · Rabbit's tomorrow promise**

At the cottage, Rabbit took both boats. “I’ll come tomorrow,” said Rabbit. “I can help with the towels.” PinPin thought that was a very good idea.

**154 · Moderate paw cleanup**

Papa wiped the day’s clay from Scooby’s paws. Scooby watched the cloth. The dog had a way of finding interesting places with very wet edges.

**155 · Show Mama gear idea**

“If we go back,” said Papa, “we’ll take what we need to wash him.” PinPin remembered the smell beside the flowers. Scooby would certainly look there again.

**156 · Lay out actual gear**

They laid out two towels, a rolled mat, the blue shampoo bottle and the wooden-backed brush. “These come with us,” said Mama. Nothing would need to appear by magic except the water.

**157 · Dry-side baby care**

Mama wiped PomPom’s cheek with his own cream cloth. “Every day we explore,” she murmured, “and every day we care.” His eyelids were growing heavy.

**158 · Map put away**

Papa put the map on its shelf. The packed supplies waited below it. Tomorrow they would finish the route—and perhaps begin with very clean paws.

**159 · Day3 sleep**

That night, the family rested beneath the quilt. Out in the forest, the chosen leaf was still. There was no shower running in the dark.
## Day 4

**160 · Final-day light**

In the morning, PomPom stirred first. Mama opened her eyes beside him. PinPin woke with the shape of the flower room still in his head.

**161 · Breakfast calmly**

At breakfast, Papa listened while PinPin described the two streams. Rabbit would be here soon. Nobody hurried the last spoonful.

**162 · Check gear list**

PinPin checked the things laid out beside the bag. “Towels, mat, bottle, brush.” Mama nodded. “And food. Explorers still get hungry.”

**163 · Pack two towels**

Papa folded the two ochre towels. One had a dark edge; the other was plain. PinPin tucked them into the dry part of the bag.

**164 · Pack bottle and brush**

Next went the closed blue bottle and the wooden-backed brush. PinPin pressed the cap to check it. Papa steadied the bag.

**165 · Roll the mat**

Rabbit arrived as they were rolling the mat, and held the loose end while PinPin tucked it in. The leaf boats were resting at home today.

**166 · Infant and family supplies**

Mama settled PomPom against her. Papa had refilled the flask and packed the food. His shoulder took the bag; her arms took the baby.

**167 · Scooby waits at step**

At the step, Scooby waited for Papa’s quiet hand. His coat was clean. His nose, however, was already looking forward to the forest.

**168 · Leave together**

They followed the known path together. “The flower room,” said PinPin. “Then the way out.” This time they would join the last lines on their map.

**169 · Forest landmark**

The forked birch appeared between the ferns. Rabbit recognised it first. Scooby recognised the smell before either child pointed.

**170 · Marker1 checked**

The first strip was still at the entrance. “We know this way now,” said Papa. They would take the strips home after their last visit.

**171 · Family at daylight lobby**

Mama and PomPom settled in the broad daylight room. “We’ll be here,” she said. PinPin showed her the last empty bend on the map.

**172 · Warm berry gallery**

The scouts passed through the amber vine gallery again. Then came the curved pool bank and the root fork. Familiar places made their footsteps slower, not faster.

**173 · Flower observation**

Papa untied the second strip as they passed the fork. In the flower room, PinPin drew the last bend toward daylight. The lilac blossoms were still growing above it.

**174 · Dog catches damp scent**

Scooby sniffed the wet root beside the flowers. It was the smell that had brought him here. He leaned a little closer.

**175 · Clay on forepaws**

His front paws pressed into the soft clay at the path’s edge. When he lifted them, both were dark and sticky.

**176 · Body brushes wet roots**

Scooby pushed his shoulder past the low wet root. A brown smear reached his chest and flank. Papa looked from the root to the dog.

**177 · More vigorous scratch**

At the other opening, Papa put the third strip in the bag. Scooby scratched at the damp bank outside. Scritch, scritch—wet earth flew onto his legs and belly.

**178 · A muddy roll**

Then he dropped one shoulder and rolled. Across the soft earth, over onto his other side, paws briefly in the air. Scooby had found something wonderful to do.

**179 · Look at the result**

He stood up covered in mud: paws, legs, belly, chest, one flank and patches of his back. Rabbit stared at the dog’s shoulder. PinPin drew his paws close.

**180 · Wait really works**

Scooby began to lower his shoulder again. “Wait,” said Papa. The dog stopped. His ears turned toward the same quiet hand he had watched at home.

**181 · Mother sees the mud**

They followed the outer forest path back around the hill. In the daylight room, Mama raised one eyebrow. PomPom peered over her arm. “Ah,” she said. “The towels have come on the right day.”

**182 · Toward chosen shower tree**

They went out by the first entrance. Papa untied the last strip and put it beside the other two. At the spreading tree, PinPin looked up at yesterday’s leaf.

**183 · Unroll carried mat**

Papa unrolled the carried mat on the flat ground beneath it. Rabbit held one corner. Scooby stood beside them, still thoroughly muddy.

**184 · Towels set aside**

The dark-edged towel went near the mat’s exit side. The plain towel stayed folded farther away. “This one needs to stay dry,” said Rabbit.

**185 · Soap and brush ready**

Papa set the bottle and brush beside the gear cloth. PinPin checked the cap once more. The leaf overhead was quiet; they had not started the water yet.

**186 · Scooby doubtful**

Scooby looked up. Then he looked at Papa. His weight moved a little backward. He had not asked the tree for a shower.

**187 · Mother/infant dry side**

Mama settled on the dry side with PomPom. “We can watch from here,” she told him. His tiny paw reached toward a patch of moving leaves.

**188 · Circle traced deliberately**

Papa steadied PinPin beneath the low branch. The child traced the gold arc carefully. Not closed yet. Not a single drop.

**189 · Falling canopy water**

He joined the ends. Water spread into soft drops below the leaf. Papa set PinPin down beside the mat. Rabbit kept the folded towel farther back.

**190 · Warm flow checked**

Papa tested the falling water again. “Just right.” Mama watched the flow and the dog. PomPom stayed snug in her arms, well away from the spray.

**191 · Nose tests droplets**

Scooby stretched his nose toward the edge of the drops. One touched his muzzle. He blinked, but Papa’s paw stayed low and patient.

**192 · One paw on mat**

The dog put one muddy paw on the mat. Then he paused. Papa waited too. There was no need to pull him.

**193 · Body under shower**

Scooby stepped farther beneath the shower. Water ran down his dirty chest and legs. PinPin watched the mud darken before it began to move.

**194 · Loosen wet soil**

Brown little runnels slipped from the coat. Papa rubbed gently where the mud was thickest. Clean water kept falling from the quiet golden circle.

**195 · Small shampoo lather**

PinPin opened the blue bottle’s attached cap. Papa used a little shampoo on the body brush. A soft lather rose on Scooby’s back, clear of his face and ears.

**196 · Brush body**

Papa brushed along the muddy coat. Scooby’s ears loosened. His eyes grew softer. PinPin could see the cream on his chest beginning to show again.

**197 · One paw cleaned**

Papa lifted one front paw just enough to wash it. The other three stayed planted. PinPin watched the spaces between the toes become clean.

**198 · Remaining paws**

The next paw needed care too, and then the back paws. Scooby shifted his weight patiently. Waiting had turned out to be useful in more places than one.

**199 · Full final rinse**

Papa rinsed the last suds away, then rinsed the brush. No mud, no soap—only a clean, very wet dog. PinPin looked along the coat before nodding.

**200 · Reverse the circle**

Papa lifted PinPin within reach again. The child traced the circle backward, all the way. Rabbit watched the water while the gold began to fade.

**201 · Last drop / source off**

One last drop fell. Papa set PinPin down. The leaf was quiet again, and everyone could see that the shower had stopped.

**202 · Step onto T1**

Scooby stepped onto the dark-edged towel. Water dripped from his chest. Papa reached for the plain towel, still folded and dry.

**203 · Still before shake**

For one moment, Scooby stood very still. His back tightened. One ear twitched. Rabbit’s eyes widened. “Oh,” said Rabbit.

**204 · Enormous shake**

WHUFF! Scooby shook from nose to tail. Drops flew in a great glittering ring. Papa turned his cheek; PinPin ducked; Rabbit’s ears took a surprising amount of water.

**205 · Wet reactions**

Papa looked at his wet paw. Rabbit looked at one dripping ear. PinPin looked at them both—and began to laugh. From the dry side, PomPom gave a small delighted squeak.

**206 · T2 dries coat**

Papa and PinPin dried Scooby with the plain towel. The dog leaned comfortably toward Papa. Both towels were wet now, but the coat was much less so.

**207 · Brush loose hair**

The rinsed brush moved through the drying coat. A small tuft of loose hair stayed in its bristles. PinPin gathered it. Scooby closed his eyes for one pleasant moment.

**208 · Pack used gear**

They closed the bottle, rolled the mat and packed the brush. Rabbit helped keep the wet towels apart from the food. Mama held PomPom while everyone checked the quiet leaf once more.

**209 · Farewell to forest helpers**

At the fern glade, Papa counted the three collected strips into the bag. “We have our map,” he said. PinPin looked back at the roots. “And Scooby found the beginning.”

**210 · Path home**

Homeward they went: one bag, two tired children, one supported baby, two parents, and four wonderfully clean dog paws. The forest rustled behind them.

**211 · Rabbit goes home**

Rabbit turned toward his own home. “The boats can come next time,” said Rabbit. PinPin waved. Today, the towels had been quite enough.

**212 · Correct full-body welcome**

At the doorway, Scooby waited. Papa looked at his clean paws. PinPin looked at his clean coat. “Come in, house dog,” said Papa. There was room for him, as there had been before.

**213 · Family account and care**

Inside, they hung the two damp towels and put the map on its shelf. Mama listened to the last of the story. “Every day we look after one another,” she said.

**214 · Yawns and settling**

The family settled beneath the familiar quilt. PomPom made a sleepy little “oo.” “Every night we rest,” whispered Mama. PinPin’s eyes closed before the room could answer.

**215 · Home at rest**

In the common room, Scooby slept on his cushion. His paws were clean, his coat was brushed, and his busy nose was quiet. Tomorrow’s smells could wait.
