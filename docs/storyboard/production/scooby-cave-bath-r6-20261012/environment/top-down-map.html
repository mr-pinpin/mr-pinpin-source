Routes and fixed chamber descriptions

Planning diagram: illustration-led authored geography, not a recovered measured site. Select a day to inspect its journey. The mother/infant station and the scout group are distinguished in the production script.

  • C0 — Root mouth: Broad root arch, forked pale root on south jamb; flowering shrub above. Daylight west. One fallen lilac blossom; M1 after day2.
  • C1 — Daylight fern lobby: Main family station. Ferns south; picnic alcove northwest; dry walkway centre; stream along north wall. Left entrance, right inner arch; flat pale boulder at C2 arch.
  • C2 — Glowberry gallery: Amber berry vines suspended north wall; lower quiet nave, not another round entrance room. Twin hanging roots at south entry; westward entrance glow behind cast.
  • C3 — Echo pool: Taller domed vault; shallow pool to north/east, broad dry south bank; filtered daylight shaft at north rim. Pale crescent pool rim and smooth concave amber ceiling; audible echo.
  • C4 — Two-stream fork: One fixed root divides shallow stream A/B; firm bank south; children observe both runs. M2 on dry-side forked root; slow eddy on stream B, no waterfall.
  • C5 — Blossom room: Large light-filled chamber, lilac hanging blossom at north skylight, fern/moss islands around clay pools. Two streams reunite under root rib west of one flat retrieval stone; scent/damp clay causes dog curiosity.
  • C6 — Daylight outlet: Open natural arch to forest; outlet stream leaves southeast, dry walkway alongside. M3 on pale leaning root; wet clay bank OUTSIDE to south causes final scratching/roll.
  • F1 — Fern/root glade: Discovery foreground; house is distant and never directly behind cave.
  • F2 — Outer forest path: Visible route around hill connecting two natural openings.
  • T0 — Chosen shower tree: Outside by west glade, no natural infinite shower. One low branch source; dry-side northwest, mat southeast, runoff southeast away from C0.
  • S1 — Outlet stream: Beaver beside outdoor stream; route to lake.
  • L0 — Known lake: Previously established lake/ducks; not a cave underground pool.
  • F0 — Forest fork: Spring branch west; cave path north; home south-west.
  • S0 — Known stone spring: Rabbit/PinPin day1 game and two canonical birds.
  • H0 — Cottage: Green front door; exterior and interior derived from published references, not this cave map.

Two shallow branches at C4 reunite in C5. The dry walking route stays on a broad bank. Skylights and luminous berry vines are the named light sources. Flowers and moss form readable islands; clear silhouettes and quiet resting spaces remain.
