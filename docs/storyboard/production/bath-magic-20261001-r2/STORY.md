# Bath chapter — playful revision R2

Status: complete 56-beat trilingual text and shot draft, commissioned revision. V1 remains untouched. All 57 selected illustrations are present. The full 56-scene sequence and final local corrections have been internally inspected. User review is pending.

## What changes

The new sequence gives the child time to enjoy what he has done: steam, a borrowed toy, paws/reflection at the rim, ripples and a pleased face. The family joins this delight before they start protecting the house. Papa’s careful step into the full bath causes another splash. PinPin’s mistargeted gesture makes the wooden duck talk; the mistake adds a friendly comic partner rather than an outside rescuer.

The water spell and duck enchantment are separate. Reversing the bath circle stops new water but does not dry the floor or silence the duck. The family still cleans together, bathes, gets wrapped in towels, and sits to breakfast. The tiny towel and the final request for water give the duck a gentle comic ending.

## Practical continuity

Papa only puts one foot in the bath while supported on the rim, then is visibly outside again at the duck greeting. Mama subsequently seats the infant above the puddles before practical rescue work. No new danger or unsupported baby is needed for the joke.

The common rug remains the one Papa lifts; do not pull the mat trapped under the bathtub. Keep two buckets and one duck. Never erase floor water with the reversed spell. Duck beak can open for speech but its body remains exactly the same wooden toy.
