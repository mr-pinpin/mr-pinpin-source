# Scooby bath — expanded day and bedtime

User-requested extension of the published Scooby chapter, adding nine original
illustrations without altering any first-edition scene, cover or image bytes.
Edition 2 has 26 pages: existing title, seven morning/play/preparation scenes,
the existing bath sequence, two additional goodnight/rest scenes and the original
sleeping ending. EN/RU/ES narration and matching downloadable PDFs.

The earlier family morning and Home Sweet Home chapters inform Mama's familiar
food preparation, clean paws, warm-water check, towels and gentle goodnight care.
The new daily refrain is newly authored, not a quotation found in those chapters.
Daily care does not imply shampooing the dog every day. Scooby eats from his own
bowl, plays after breakfast, splashes in a shallow rain puddle and rolls in damp
leaves; this supplies the missing reason for his bath. Sleep means resting tired
paws and quieting down after the day, with another day of play ahead.

Outdoor scenes use a garden clearing without house geometry. Two indoor additions
use recorded jobs and fresh reusable Blender renders of the same measured camera
as the preserved final page. Geometry references govern architecture; prior book
illustrations supply style/identity only. The cushion is a removable story prop.
Heavy renders and PDFs stay on external VPS storage because the mini is unavailable.

`generation-records.json` preserves exact built-in imagegen prompts, input hashes,
original tool paths and visual selection status. Initial muddy breakfast/reminder
candidates are retained separately and corrected before publication. Native PNGs
are retained locally and archived through verified asset synchronization; full
1536×1024 WebPs use quality 86 without resizing/cropping. Recipes and provenance
stay in Git. No software MIT grant is applied to the illustrations or story; see
root content notices. No rights are claimed in unprotectable AI output.

Published at https://mr-pinpin.github.io/storyboard/?story=scooby-bath&lang=en.
`publication.json` and `live-reader-proof.json` record the verified deployment and
all 26 pages at 390px and 1440px in EN/RU/ES. Prior release
records, archive objects, first-edition PDF records and image masters remain intact.
