# Bath chapter R4 — four days of water

Preproduction only: 80 illustrated story beats and one proposed cover, with short English, Russian and Spanish narration. No R4 story images are generated, selected or published by this plan. R3 remains unchanged. Previous images are explicitly labelled composition candidates, never presumed to match new days, expressions, water routing or the plastic duck.

Day1 (01–38) keeps the first water spell, real Papa jump, shared family bath and underwater/near-lens views. Papa acknowledges the spill before playing; the family then builds a temporary route outside and deals with evening drips. Day2 (39–52) repeats inconvenient household tasks. A failed stopping spell makes flowers; their brief beauty becomes a blocked channel. Day3 (53–67) brings the genuinely unexpected talking toy. The duck loves the water while the family is tired. Day4 (68–80) uses an observed effect to discover the inverse circle and earn quiet relief.

The visual emotional range matters as much as the text. Resting mouths, lowered shoulders, concentration, mild irritation, disbelief and sleepy eyes replace indiscriminate smiles. PomPom can still enjoy a sound while Mama keeps working; the duck can be delighted while Papa has had enough. Nobody humiliates or scolds PinPin.

The practical route follows the existing house: temporary collection mouth and descending wooden trough supports, from the bath spill through the existing bathroom/common/front-door route into the garden. Residual drips remain believable chores. The bedroom stays dry behind its closed door and a splash towel. The large rust bath rug never leaves its place beneath the heavy tub. A separate small blue/cream common-room mat may be lifted. No new room, loft, drain or permanent geometry is invented.

The ordinary toy is soft yellow plastic with white eyes and black pupils. There is no borrowing, delivery, talking or closeup hero introduction before56. Any old wooden duck, including a tiny background one, prevents automatic art reuse.

The solution is earned visually:49 a short reverse trace falters the stream;50 an ordinary request interrupts the incomplete motion;66–67 PinPin remembers it;70–72 he repeats the short motion and watches the stream return when he stops;73–75 he deliberately completes the inverse. Mama notices and confirms rather than teaching the answer. Stopping the source does not remove water already present or silence the living toy.
