# R6 audit against the author's feedback

## What was planned, and why it was insufficient

R6 had 215 authored narrative beats, three-language captions, subplot/setup/payoff
tables, an object/holder/soil ledger and seven named cave spaces. That work was
real. It chiefly verified order and continuity; it did not demonstrate a strong
mystery, varied acting, or matching geography in the resulting pictures.

The cave plan is a bent chain C0–C6. It includes a shallow echo pool and a stream
fork, then an outdoor lake. It has no substantial underground lake, caretaker
history, meaningful concealment or contested resource. Mapping the path itself
became the payoff. Returning and mapping again fills pages without proportionate
changes in what the children understand or decide.

Named rooms and anchors did not lock translated cameras. Most generated shots
still present the cast together facing the viewer, with new arches/berries as a
backdrop. Doorway reversals and new camera origins were left to illustration
inference. A diagram and correct node name cannot prove a visible sightline.

## Scooby's home identity

Compare approved `bath-magic/r17/scene-03i.webp` and
`bath-magic/r24/scene-05g2.webp`: mature elongated tawny muzzle, reddish forehead
without a vertical pale blaze, triangular drooping ears, cream chest, textured
reddish body, long sturdy limbs, large adult proportions and curved tail.

R6 reader pages **53, 214 and 216** show the most obvious indoor drift: a
rounder puppy face, pale central facial blaze, pale paws, different ear length
and/or reduced adult scale. The other home scenes were compared on a labelled
20-image diagnostic contact sheet; exterior dog identity is generally closer,
although scale and acting remain subject to shot review.

The recorded generation for scene215/reader216 used only `r6-001-v1.png`, a new
breakfast image. It supplied no approved Scooby anatomy. A lengthy identity
sentence cannot compensate for an absent visual character reference. New
generated images used as subsequent identity authorities can propagate drift.

Corrections use approved dog references independently of the sleep pose and
existing room composition. A successful identity edit does not certify that the
inherited room matches the measured house.

## Acting

Script directions across 215 scenes contain attention/focus/concentration terms
in 105 scenes, worry/puzzlement/thoughtful terms in31, surprise/awe in9 and
explicit pleasure/play/amusement terms in5. Categories overlap, and word counts
do not measure the rendered faces, but they explain a strong directional bias.

The instruction to remove automatic smiles was repeatedly reinforced, while
ordinary ease, humour, anticipation, pride, absorption and pleasure received
little positive direction. The corrective instruction then became a new
default face: knitted/raised brows and solemn attention. Sleep pages still have
high arched brows. Acting should be a specific response to a concrete stimulus,
not a permanent register attached to a character or an anti-smile checklist.

## Revision requirements

1. Every discovery changes a hypothesis or a decision and has visible evidence.
2. The lake has a mapped position, occluding approach, source, outlet, banks,
   water level and persistent lights. A beautiful room alone is not a revelation.
3. Character anatomy, place geometry, palette and acting have separate references.
4. Each camera has an origin, target, height/FOV, forward/reverse axis, visible
   landmarks and forbidden unseen landmarks. Inspect adjacent shots together.
5. Each visible actor has an action, object of attention and emotional change.
6. Judge sequence quality against Bath Magic, not page count or passing hash checks.
