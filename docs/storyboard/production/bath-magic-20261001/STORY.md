## Magic and water

PinPin traces one clockwise gold circle over the bath. A small fountain fills it. Tracing the same circle backwards erases it and stops new water. The floor stays wet. Mama asks him to show what he did; PinPin has the idea himself. There are no enchanted buckets or summoned helpers.

Keep the circle horizontally fixed near the east end of the bath and visible just above the water; its height follows the rising surface. The paw close-ups at scenes09,26,28 share the same proposed camera so reversal is readable. Gold movement and erasure explain the direction without painted arrows or instructional text.

The flood is shallow, around their paws. PomPom stays above it in an explicitly supported seat, while adults move belongings and remain nearby. Comedy comes from a duck floating to breakfast, more spills than buckets, and the rug’s final splash. Cleanup genuinely reduces water. Mama checks and supervises the eventual bath. There is no stern lecture.
