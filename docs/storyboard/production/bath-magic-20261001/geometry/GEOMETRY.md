# Bath chapter geometry and closing illustrations

Nine canonical camera guides rendered on the mini with tools/locations/cli.py, then prepared and visually inspected. Jobs and `camera-guides.json` live beside this report. Heavy renders, prepared inputs, pinned build and .blend remain in the external pack `geometry/` directory. Canonical plan was not edited.

The guide cameras are bath-wide, bath-child, bath-overhead, bath-door, bath-circle, common-table, common-kitchen, common-hall, bedroom. Bath-circle is the exact proposed scene09/26/28 right-shoulder transform. Prepared manifests validate source inputs and output bytes. These checks do not prove the illustrations obey every coordinate: book reference composition sometimes dominates the grey guide.

Scenes25–36 were generated with the built-in image_gen__imagegen tool, one call per asset. Twenty-one generated assets: twelve initial illustrations plus nine versioned corrections; all masters and prompts retained. Scene26v2 and scene28v1 derive directly from scene09 to preserve the most important creation/reversal view. No image API fallback or deterministic compositing was used for artwork. WebP copies are format conversion only.

Selected versions: 25v2,26v2,27v1,28v1,29v2,30v2,31v1,32v2,33v1,34v3,35v2,36v2. All selected frames were visually inspected. Exact prompts and tool/input/output provenance are in production `prompts/` and `generation-records/`, mirrored into external pack.

Corrections address circle camera match, decreasing fountain size, accidental extra cast, cup stream landing inside pail, lower bathwater, carved wooden duck identity, dark window hole and modern bathroom fixtures leaking from geometry. Remaining minor limits: exact water percentages and limb parts hidden by objects cannot be certified; scene31 rug shape differs from planned oval; scene33 shows Papa carrying rug before taking it outside. The final draft is ready for user feedback, not approval or publication.
